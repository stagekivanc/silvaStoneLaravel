1. Legal nature and scope

Article 10 of Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”), titled “Obligation of the Data Controller to Inform”, requires that data subjects be informed when their personal data are obtained. Under the KVKK, Acarkon Entegre Ağaç San. ve Tic. A.Ş. is the “data controller”. Accordingly, this notice informs data subjects about the identity of the data controller, the purposes of processing, the parties to whom data may be transferred and the purposes of transfer, the legal grounds and methods of collection, and the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK (including access, correction, deletion and anonymisation requests).

Article 3(1)(ı) of the KVKK defines the data controller as the natural or legal person who determines the purposes and means of processing personal data and is responsible for establishing and managing the data filing system. In this context, the data controller of the website you visit is Acarkon Entegre Ağaç San. ve Tic. A.Ş.
