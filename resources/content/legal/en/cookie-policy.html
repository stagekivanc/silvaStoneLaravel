Cookie policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Acarkon Entegre Ağaç San. ve Tic. A.Ş. (“Acarkon”, “we”) uses cookies and similar technologies on the Silva Stone / Acarkon website. For general personal data practices, please also read our Privacy policy and Privacy notice.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They do not damage your computer and do not contain viruses. Cookies used on our website do not, by themselves, reveal your identity; they help the site function, remember preferences and understand aggregate usage.

2. Types of cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for core site functions such as security, language and session continuity.
  • Performance / analytics cookies: help us understand how pages are used so we can improve content and navigation (typically aggregated and non-identifying).
  • Functionality cookies: remember choices such as language or filter preferences where enabled.
  • Marketing cookies: used only where lawful consent is obtained, to measure campaigns or deliver relevant messages.

3. Purposes of use

Cookies are used principally during a session for anonymous statistical evaluation, usability improvements and, where applicable, remembering user preferences. In certain sections of the site, additional cookies may be used; you will be informed when those sections are accessed.

4. Managing cookies

You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality. Browser help pages explain how to manage cookies for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and other browsers.

5. Third-party services

Our pages may include maps, video or analytics services provided by third parties. Those providers may set their own cookies subject to their privacy policies. Acarkon is not responsible for the cookie practices of external sites linked from our website.

6. Retention

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a defined period or until deleted by you. Retention periods depend on the cookie type and the purpose for which it was placed.
